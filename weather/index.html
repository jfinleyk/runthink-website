The temperature where you actually are.

Official stations can sit miles away. We pull from several free public sources at once, blend the readings, and give you an honest number for your activity right where you are.

Enter a location or tap the target to read the stations near you.

Readings come from the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, OpenWeather, and Visual Crossing. We show every source that reports and blend them; with four or more we drop the single highest and lowest first. Weather data by OpenWeather and Visual Crossing.
